### Patch Changes

- [#657](https://github.com/pie-framework/pie-elements-ng/pull/657) [`81c8941`](https://github.com/pie-framework/pie-elements-ng/commit/81c8941e768b04403d47620ee2d5ce835def8b95) Thanks [@chillenious](https://github.com/chillenious)! - Reads Arabic letters as variables, letters of any script inside `\text{}`, and the Arabic comma `،` as a list separator.

- [#665](https://github.com/pie-framework/pie-elements-ng/pull/665) [`99bb5f8`](https://github.com/pie-framework/pie-elements-ng/commit/99bb5f8eabf168e686af739cc65ba45e0d744b8d) Thanks [@CarlaCostea](https://github.com/CarlaCostea)! - Symbolic mode compares answers made only of numbers and arithmetic exactly, however many digits they have, so `123456789.00001` no longer matches `123456789` and `12345678901234567` no longer matches `12345678901234568` (PIE-905, PIE-1418). The small tolerance for floating-point noise now applies only to answers with a function, π, an angle or a variable, so `\sin(30°)` still matches `0.5`.

  Score changes to expect when re-scoring: a rounded decimal no longer matches the exact fraction (`0.6666666666666667` vs `\frac{2}{3}`, `0.3333333333333333` vs `\frac{1}{3}`), and a very small number no longer matches `0` (`10^{-20}` or `0.000000000000001` vs `0`).

- [#708](https://github.com/pie-framework/pie-elements-ng/pull/708) [`9845305`](https://github.com/pie-framework/pie-elements-ng/commit/9845305219ed56ce012d4650b68863038f97a974) Thanks [@CarlaCostea](https://github.com/CarlaCostea)! - In literal mode with `ignoreOrder`, a product of several fractions now matches the same fractions in another order, also with the factors inside them reordered, as `\frac{c}{d}\cdot\frac{bz}{2}` matches `\frac{zb}{2}\cdot\frac{c}{d}` (PIE-1541). Terms and factors are sorted inside out, so the sums of a product may be reordered inside too, as `(b+z)(c+w)` matches `(z+b)(w+c)`. 1.4.3 rejected these, so re-scoring can also turn answers like this from incorrect to correct. Sorting only reorders them: a numerator or denominator moved to another fraction still doesn't match, so neither `\frac{4}{3}\cdot\frac{2}{5}` nor `\frac{2\cdot4}{3\cdot5}` matches `\frac{2}{3}\cdot\frac{4}{5}`. A single fraction still takes the factors beside it into its numerator, as in 1.4.3, so `2\cdot\frac{4}{5}` matches `\frac{2\cdot4}{5}`.

- [#650](https://github.com/pie-framework/pie-elements-ng/pull/650) [`67e54ad`](https://github.com/pie-framework/pie-elements-ng/commit/67e54ad81d769577ae69b681e1439b7102ee35b2) Thanks [@chillenious](https://github.com/chillenious)! - Symbolic mode no longer scores inequalities with fractions differently once an equation has been compared earlier in the same page or process.

- [#694](https://github.com/pie-framework/pie-elements-ng/pull/694) [`c6477cc`](https://github.com/pie-framework/pie-elements-ng/commit/c6477cc179b3924b6e2a1c85f2e12dfcdb89342b) Thanks [@CarlaCostea](https://github.com/CarlaCostea)! - Symbolic mode no longer takes seconds to minutes to compare high powers and products of sums, such as `(x+1)^{8}` or `(a+b+c+d)^{3}`: past a size limit, `@pie-framework/math-validation` compares the two expressions at sample points instead of multiplying them out (PIE-1532).

  Score changes to expect when re-scoring: past that limit, some correct answers that were rejected now match, such as `(x+y)^{2}(x+y)^{3}` for `(x+y)^{5}` and `(x+1)^{7.0}` for `(1+x)^{7}`. A wrong answer that differs from the key by less than about 1e-10 of its value now matches too, such as `(x+100)^{6}+1` for `(x+100)^{6}`. With a variable in a denominator, the denominators must also be zero at the same values, complex ones included (PIE-1540). So `(x+1)^{5}x^{-1}` matches `\frac{(x+1)^{5}}{x}`, and a factor may be written out, as `\frac{(x+1)^{5}}{x^{3}+2x^{2}}` matches `\frac{(x+1)^{5}}{x^{2}(x+2)}`, while a form with a removable hole, such as `\frac{x^{6}-1}{x-1}(x+1)^{4}`, still does not match `(x^{5}+x^{4}+x^{3}+x^{2}+x+1)(x+1)^{4}`. 1.4.3 compared the multiplied-out denominators instead, so some correct answers it rejected now match, such as `(x+1)^{8}+\frac{1}{x-1}+\frac{1}{x^{2}-1}` for `(x+1)^{8}+\frac{x+2}{x^{2}-1}` and `-\frac{(x+1)^{5}}{x-1}` for `\frac{(x+1)^{5}}{1-x}`. A fraction inside a denominator can still hide a hole, as in 1.4.3: `\frac{(x+1)^{8}}{\frac{1}{x}}` matches `x(x+1)^{8}`. A sum under a variable exponent, such as `(x+1)^{y}`, keeps the verdict it gets without multiplying out. The existing fixtures keep their verdicts in both argument orders.

- [#657](https://github.com/pie-framework/pie-elements-ng/pull/657) [`859a118`](https://github.com/pie-framework/pie-elements-ng/commit/859a1182873e32d272be3996ab2b5b21e059b074) Thanks [@chillenious](https://github.com/chillenious)! - Reads `{,}` and `\,` between digit groups as thousands separators, `\dfrac` and `\tfrac` as fractions, and `2\ \frac{1}{2}` as a mixed number; `1,0000` is no longer read as `1000·0`, nor `00` as not a number. `compareNumbersByValue` compares exact values, beyond double precision.

- [#698](https://github.com/pie-framework/pie-elements-ng/pull/698) [`c335d4f`](https://github.com/pie-framework/pie-elements-ng/commit/c335d4f0e08dd47c845c173adb94a9619ba3474a) Thanks [@CarlaCostea](https://github.com/CarlaCostea)! - Ships the license notice of math-validation's LaTeX parser, which is derived from math-expressions and used under Apache-2.0, in the `THIRD_PARTY_LICENSES.txt` of math-validation's build and of every math-inline and math-templated bundle that includes the parser (PIE-1535).

- [#702](https://github.com/pie-framework/pie-elements-ng/pull/702) [`ebc82a5`](https://github.com/pie-framework/pie-elements-ng/commit/ebc82a53824fda260f0e2ab52fc918ba027801e7) Thanks [@CarlaCostea](https://github.com/CarlaCostea)! - `@pie-framework/math-validation` no longer accepts some wrong answers that sorting made identical to the key (PIE-1541), in symbolic mode and in literal mode with `ignoreOrder`. A product with a fraction or a negative power among its factors kept only the single symbols and numbers beside it, so `(x+2)x^{-1}` matched `(x+1)x^{-1}`. A nested negation, quotient or power was merged into its parent, so `-(-x)` matched `-x`, `\frac{\frac{b}{c}}{a}` matched `\frac{a}{\frac{b}{c}}` and `2^{3^{x}}` matched `(3^{x})^{2}`. 1.4.3 has the same fault, so re-scoring can turn answers like these from correct to incorrect.
