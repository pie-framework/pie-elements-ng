Accept typed input while a math keypad button has keyboard focus: letters, digits and symbols go into the math entry area, or the math answer block, at the cursor, as if typed there, and Backspace and Delete delete there; focus stays on the button. Space, Enter, Tab, the arrow keys and Ctrl, Cmd and Alt shortcuts keep their keypad roles.
