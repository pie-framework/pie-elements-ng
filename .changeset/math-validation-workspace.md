Now developed in pie-elements-ng and published as an ES module only. Adds the `literal.compareNumbersByValue` option, under which a single number matches only a single number of equal value, and exports the `LiteralOpts` and `Mode` types. Licensed MIT, with the LaTeX parser derived from math-expressions under Apache-2.0. Depends on mathjs 15, up from 9.
