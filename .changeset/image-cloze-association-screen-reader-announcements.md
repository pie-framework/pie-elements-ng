Screen reader announcements follow the image-cloze-association spec. After the prompt, a hidden intro describes the image, how many drop areas it has and how many answer choices each holds, whether choices can be reused, the keys to use, what each drop area contains and which answer choices are still available. A focused answer choice is named "Answer choice: …", with the drop area it sits in, in place of dnd-kit's "draggable". While a choice is selected, each drop area and the pool is announced with what ENTER will do there, and a selection, placement, move, removal or cancel is announced as it happens.
