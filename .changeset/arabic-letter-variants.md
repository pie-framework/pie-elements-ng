Authors can let a response accept Arabic spelling variants (hamza and madda alef forms, tashkeel, ه for ة, ي for ى): one switch in simple-cloze, a switch plus a per-area toggle in explicit-constructed-response. Off by default. Settings-panel toggles take optional help text.
