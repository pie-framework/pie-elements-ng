Add Tab/Shift+Tab placement and click-to-select/click-to-place to categorize. Tab cycles a picked-up choice through the enabled categories in reading order and then the choices pool; arrow-key dragging is unchanged. Clicking a choice selects it and clicking a category places it, so placement no longer requires a drag (PIE-804, PIE-797)
