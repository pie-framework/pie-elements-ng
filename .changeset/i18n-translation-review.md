Corrections to seven of the multiple-choice interface catalogs from a review by three independent expert reviewers per locale, applied only where at least two agreed. French renames the multi-select heading `Question à réponses multiples`: `Question à choix multiples` was a homophone of the single-answer heading (the final s is silent), so a screen-reader user could not hear which of the two question types was announced. Welsh takes the Termiadur term `Cwestiwn aml-ddewis` for the multiple-choice heading and `Cwestiwn dewis lluosog` for multi-select, keeping the pair audibly distinct, and puts the singular noun after 5–6 in `maxSelections.many` like every other plural group in the suite. Japanese counts answer choices with 個 in `maxSelections.other`, matching its minimum-selection strings; Korean says 답안 there, matching its answer-choices group name; Thai aligns `maxSelections.other` with its sibling `เลือก … รายการ` constraints; Portuguese names the answer-choices group `Alternativas de resposta`, the Brazilian assessment term pie-players already uses; Spanish drops the pre-2010 accent on `Solo`. Keys, placeholders and plural shapes are unchanged.
