Screen reader announcements follow the categorize spec, for categories in a list and in a matrix. After the prompt, a hidden intro describes the categories (or the matrix and its row and column labels), how many answer choices each holds, whether choices can be reused, the keys to use, what each category contains and which answer choices are still available. A focused answer choice is named "Answer choice: …", with its category, in place of dnd-kit's "draggable". While a choice is selected, each category and the pool is announced with what ENTER will do there, and a selection, placement, move, removal or cancel is announced as it happens. After a keyboard drop the choice that landed takes focus, so the learner keeps their place, also when a reusable choice leaves a copy in the pool; what the drop did is read in that choice's name until focus leaves it. Everything is announced through one live region, as in match-list, so announcements no longer compete in JAWS. The same words reach NVDA and JAWS in browse mode, where ENTER is a click: while a choice is click-selected, a category or the pool that takes focus is named by what ENTER does there, and ESC ends the selection. A choice that displaces another reads "To make room for it, answer choice … was removed." in a list as well as in a matrix.
