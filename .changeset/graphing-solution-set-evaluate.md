Evaluate in graphing-solution-set now follows graphing's design: each line end and shaded region wears a check, cross or missing icon in the verdict's colour, a line the student did not draw is thin and dashed, and a key explains the marks. A line's solid or dashed style is judged apart from its position, against the key line it lies on or the one in its slot: a neutral pill beside the line shows the style drawn against the style expected. A line on a key line in the wrong style is drawn as correctly placed, still scores as incorrect, and is no longer also reported as a missing line.
